How to explore this dashboard · definitions and coverage

View screening rules and cohort audit

Start with Country ECI to compare countries and years; every domain has a dataset selector (Product: HS92 1995–2024, HS 2002 and HS 2007 nomenclatures; Technology and Knowledge: source, counting rule and year basis). The AI economic complexity tab shows the unscreened AECI / AIAP / AECP indices and the RCA of the WTO list of AI-enabling goods. Use Activity complexity (PCI) for products, technology classes and knowledge subfields. Explore annual networks in Activity space, for the latest two available years in each dataset, then inspect sorted country–activity matrices and null-model comparisons in Nestedness.

ECI describes country complexity in each domain. PCI is product complexity; this project stores the equivalent activity index for all three domains as ACI. Scores are relative to each dataset and year, so changes do not measure absolute capability growth. Missing observations remain missing. Knowledge corresponds to the Science datasets; technology reflects the project's PatentsView data coverage.

Latest means latest available, not necessarily complete: recent patent and publication years can have incomplete coverage. Product activity descriptions are representative HS6 descriptions within each HS4 code.

RCA ≥ 1 identifies specialization within the screened reference population. Diversity counts a country's specialized activities; ubiquity counts countries specialized in an activity. Activity-space links represent proximity; the displayed network uses a maximum spanning forest plus strong links. Detailed figures cover only the latest two available years per dataset: Product 2023–2024; Technology and Knowledge 2024–2025. Metric trajectories retain all available years.

Nestedness examines whether less diversified countries' activity baskets tend to be subsets of more diversified baskets. Read each observed measure against its own null model. sNODF includes equal-degree pairs; temperature score is 1 − T/100 (higher is more nested); nSNES is the leading singular value divided by the square root of the number of links. Data availability and country coverage vary by year and dataset. RCA, ECI/ACI, fitness, proximity and nestedness are recalculated after annual screening of the upstream activity matrices. These are project estimates, not official country rankings. Parquet files and figures are loaded from the EC repository. Latest years can differ across domains; the year is displayed beside every detailed view.

AI economic complexity · unscreened project indices

Loading…

AECI = Σ over the 103 AI-enabling goods of M · q: the number of listed goods a country exports with RCA > 1, each weighted by its normalised complexity q (PCI of all ~5,000 HS6 products rescaled to 0–1); reported as a z-score across the year's valid countries. AIAP places the goods a country does not yet hold by relatedness density ρ to its whole export portfolio and by q. AECP is the mean of ρ · q over those candidates (raw G in 0–1, z-score across countries): how favourable the remaining AI goods are on average. Bundle RCA is the RCA of the 103 goods taken as one product. Unlike the other tabs these values are computed on the raw BACI matrices (no screening) and use the strict RCA > 1 rule; highlighted countries are the ones selected in the Country ECI tab.

Original project visualizations

Figures from the AI-Economic-Complexity branch: AI-enabling trade, complexity indices, product-space networks, patent–product concordance, and the analysis report. These unscreened results retain the notebooks' country and year selections. Interactive figures have their own controls.

Indices use strict RCA > 1; product-space holdings and concordance use RCA ≥ 1. AIAP charts distinguish normalized complexity q from PCI. Report comparisons are descriptive, and use the product domain only.

Products linked by proximity φ (the smaller of the two conditional probabilities of specialisation in one product given specialisation in the other, Hidalgo et al. 2007), computed on the raw BACI HS 2007 matrices. The drawn backbone is the maximum spanning tree of the proximity pooled over 2007–2023 plus the four strongest links at or above 0.55 (HS4) / 0.60 (HS6) per product; layout and Louvain communities are fixed across years, so only colours, hover values and the country overlay change with the year slider. Node size follows PageRank in the pooled proximity. Hover a node for its code, description, chapter and community; the listed goods at HS6 also show the year's PCI and ubiquity.
